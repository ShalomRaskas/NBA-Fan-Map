# Geographic data and external resources

World outlines and label anchors are derived from world-atlas 2 / Natural Earth data (public domain).
US state outlines are derived from us-atlas 3 / US Census Bureau geographic data (public domain).
The map displays attribution in its interface.

Leaflet is loaded at runtime from unpkg (Leaflet 1.9.4, BSD-2-Clause).
City names and coordinates are supplied by Open-Meteo Geocoding, using GeoNames data (CC BY 4.0).
Team logos are referenced from ESPN's public CDN and remain the property of their respective owners. Referencing them does not grant trademark rights or imply NBA or ESPN endorsement.
Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts: Barlow Condensed and DM Sans (SIL Open Font License).
